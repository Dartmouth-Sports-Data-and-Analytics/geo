About the heat maps

Ivy Roster Construction

Data

  • From the public roster pages on the schools' websites
  • Tracked in academic years, 2016-17 to now
  • One US map per school, shaded by athletes per state (each athlete counted once per state)
  • International athletes are totaled but not drawn
  • Athletes whose hometown could not be located are left out, so totals run slightly low
  • Men's, women's and coed teams are combined in cross country, track and field and sailing

Choices

  • Seasons
    • 2020-21: COVID year, many teams thin
    • The current season: partial until winter and spring rosters are posted
  • Teams
    • Harvard men's rowing 2019-20: not on the site
    • Yale men's golf 2023-24: team page is blank, so it is rebuilt from the neighboring seasons (may be incomplete, included in Yale's counts)

Filters

  • Seasons: any season, or Latest 5 (selected at first)
  • Sports and regions (by hometown): every varsity sport, and seven US regions plus International / Other; all on at first
  • A school with no matching team shows a grey "No team" card
  • Hover or tap a state for its count, share, rank and sports

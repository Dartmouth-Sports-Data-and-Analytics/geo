About the heat maps

How many athletes each school draws from each state.

The data

  • One US map per school, each state shaded by its number of athletes, from the eight schools' public roster pages, 2016-17 to now.
  • A season is the academic year: 2024 means 2024-25. Each person counts once per state; international athletes are totaled but not drawn.
  • Cross Country, Track and Field and Sailing combine the men's, women's and coed teams; every other sport stays separate.

Gaps and caveats

  • School sites sometimes serve another season's roster; we drop seasons that copy one another.
  • 2020-21 is thin (many seasons were cancelled), and the current season is partial until winter and spring rosters are posted.
  • Harvard men's rowing 2019-20 doesn't exist on the site, so it is left out.
  • Yale men's golf 2023-24: the team page is blank, so the roster is rebuilt from the seasons either side (athletes on both rosters, juniors who likely finished as seniors, sophomores who likely started as first-years). It is a minimum. Included in Yale's counts.

Filters

  • Seasons (latest 5 at first), Sports and Regions combine; a school with no matching team shows a grey "No team" card.
  • Hover or tap a state for its count, share, rank and sports.

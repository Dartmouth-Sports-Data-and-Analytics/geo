About the data

Where Ivy League varsity athletes come from, and what to know before reading the numbers.

What this is

  • One dot per person, built from the public roster pages on the eight schools' athletics sites, 2016-17 to now.
  • A season is the academic year: 2024 means 2024-25, for every sport.
  • A person on two teams is one dot with both sports listed.
  • Hometowns are shown as the schools publish them. A few can't be located, so those athletes have no dot.

Roster problems and how we handled them

  • Wrong-season pages. A school site sometimes returns another season's roster, or its default page, instead of an error. We pick the right page form for each team, and drop any season that copies another one or whose class labels didn't move up.
  • 2020-21 is thin. Many fall and winter seasons were cancelled. Harvard and Princeton have about a third fewer athletes than usual.
  • The current season is partial. Winter and spring teams often haven't posted new rosters, so Dartmouth, Penn and Princeton dip until they do.
  • Harvard men's rowing 2019-20 doesn't exist on the site (it serves the current roster instead), so that season is left out.
  • Yale cross country and track and field 2022-23 have class labels a year behind at the source. Names and hometowns are right, and class isn't used on either map.
  • Fifth-year athletes (mostly the COVID extra year) can appear for five seasons.

Inferred team: Yale men's golf 2023-24

Yale's team page for this season is blank, although the players have individual pages. We rebuilt it from the seasons on either side:

  • athletes on both the 2022-23 and 2024-25 rosters;
  • juniors in 2022-23 who aren't on the 2024-25 roster (so, seniors who graduated);
  • sophomores in 2024-25 who weren't on the 2022-23 roster (so, first-years).

This misses anyone who was on the team only that year, so the count is a minimum. Those seasons carry an asterisk in a dot's popup.

How sports are grouped

  • Cross Country, Track and Field and Sailing each combine the men's, women's and coed teams into one sport. Harvard and Columbia publish single combined pages for cross country and track.
  • Everything else stays separate by gender, and rowing stays separate by heavyweight and lightweight.

Roster map filters

  • Schools: none selected at first. Select all, Clear all, or DHYP (Dartmouth, Harvard, Yale, Princeton). With exactly one school on, Lines from campus draws a line to each hometown.
  • Years: the latest 5 seasons at first. Select all shows every season; 2020-21 is thin, so a separate Latest 10 isn't offered.
  • Regions: CA / TX / FL, New England, Mid-Atlantic, Southeast, Midwest, Mountain West & Southwest, Northwest & Pacific, and International / Other, each with a count.
  • Sports and a name search (ignores accents and case; every word must match).
  • A dot shows if the person has a selected sport in a selected season.

Heat map filters

  • Seasons (latest 5 at first), Sports and Regions.
  • Each school has its own card and color. A state is shaded by its number of athletes, and each person counts once per state.
  • International athletes are totaled on the card but not drawn. A school with no team for the selection shows a grey "No team" card.

About the roster map

Ivy Roster Construction

Data

  • From the public roster pages on the schools' websites
  • Tracked in academic years, 2016-17 to now
  • One dot per athlete and hometown
    • Click a dot to see name, school, hometown, and each sport with its seasons
  • Men's, women's and coed teams are combined in cross country, track and field and sailing

Choices

  • Seasons with duplicate rosters are removed
  • Thin or missing seasons/teams
    • Seasons
      • 2019-2021: COVID years, roughly
      • 2026: partial until winter and spring rosters are posted
    • Teams
      • Harvard men's rowing 2019-20: not on the site
      • Yale men's golf 2023-24: team page is blank, so it is rebuilt from the neighboring seasons (may be incomplete, marked * in popups)

Filters

  • Defaults: no schools selected (so the map starts empty), the latest 5 seasons, all sports and regions
  • Schools: any of the eight Ivy League schools, or DHYP (Dartmouth, Harvard, Yale and Princeton)
  • Years: any season from 2016-17 to now, or Latest 5
  • Regions (by hometown): CA / TX / FL, New England, Mid-Atlantic, Southeast, Midwest, Mountain West & Southwest, Northwest & Pacific and International / Other
  • Sports: any varsity sport on the schools' sites
  • Search: find an athlete by name
  • Athletes appear if they match at least one selection in each filter

About the roster map

Ivy Roster Construction

Data

  • From the public roster pages on the schools' websites
  • Tracked in academic years, 2016-17 to now
  • One dot per athlete and hometown
    • Click a dot to see name, school, hometown, and each sport with its seasons
    • Dots sit at the center of the hometown, not an address; athletes from the same town fan out in a small spiral
  • Athletes whose hometown could not be located are left out, so totals run slightly low
  • Men's, women's and coed teams are combined in cross country, track and field and sailing

Choices

  • Seasons
    • 2020-21: COVID year, many teams thin
    • The current season: partial until winter and spring rosters are posted
  • Teams
    • Harvard men's rowing 2019-20: not on the site
    • Yale men's golf 2023-24: team page is blank, so it is rebuilt from the neighboring seasons (may be incomplete, marked * in popups)

Filters

  • Schools: eight Ivy League schools, or DHYP (Dartmouth, Harvard, Yale and Princeton); none selected at first, so the map starts empty
  • Years: any season, or Latest 5 (selected at first)
  • Regions (by hometown) and sports: seven US regions plus International / Other, and every varsity sport; all on at first
  • Search: find an athlete by name
  • Athletes appear if they match at least one selection in each filter
